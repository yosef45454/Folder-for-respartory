05The Problem—Why ads stop working

Your customer
acquisition challenge

It's not your budget. It's not the platform. It's one of these three things.

Issue 01
01
— Issue 01

Creative
fatigue.

Your best ads have an expiry date. The moment your audience has seen the same creative enough times, they stop reacting. CTR drops. CPC climbs. CAC gets higher until the campaign isn't profitable anymore. Most businesses don't catch this until they've already wasted weeks of budget on dead creative.

Issue 02
02
— Issue 02

No clear
strategy.

Running ads without a strategy is just donating money to Meta. Which angle do you lead with? What emotion makes your customer stop scrolling? What offer gets the click? Without answers, every ad is a guess — and guessing with real money gets expensive fast.

Issue 03
03
— Issue 03

No system to
find winners.

The difference between a weak ad and a winning ad can be 10x in performance. The only way to find your winners is through a disciplined testing process — new concepts in, data out, iterate fast. Without it, your best-performing ads never get discovered.
